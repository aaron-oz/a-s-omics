* Grid and notation

Bin indices: i = (x - 15025)/50 + 1, from 1 to 200; j = (y - 11525)/50 + 1, from 1 to 130; x, y
in chip (DNB spot) units. The single source of truth for every region and slice is
=design/design-regions.rds=, written by =design/design-regions.R=; later scripts read it and never
re-derive regions.

For gene g, lambda_g(s) is the rate at bin s (expected count per UMI). For a ligand-receptor pair
with ligand L and receptor R, the rate-scale signaling field is M(s) = min(lambda_L(s),
lambda_R(s)). Posterior draws d = 1, ..., D: lambda_L^(d) and lambda_R^(d) come from separate fits,
so they are independent, and M^(d)(s) = min(lambda_L^(d)(s), lambda_R^(d)(s)).

* Quantities compared (all on the rate scale, compared as log ratios)

1. *Areal average*, same field: A_R^(d) = (1/|R|) sum over s in R of M^(d)(s). Contrast between
   regions R1, R2: log A_R1^(d) - log A_R2^(d). Computed from bin-level draws of both partners,
   because the average of a minimum is not the minimum of averages.
2. *Shape normalization*, cross field: S^(d)(s) = M^(d)(s) / (mean over all 26,000 bins of
   M^(d)), computed within each draw so the uncertainty of the tissue average is carried
   through. Cross-field regional contrast in region R: log A_R(S1)^(d) - log A_R(S2)^(d), i.e.
   "is field 1 relatively more concentrated in R than field 2?".
3. *Slices*: (a) same field, log M^(d)(p) - log(mean over the slice of M^(d)) at slice position
   p, asking whether the field varies along the slice; (b) cross field, log S1^(d)(p) - log
   S2^(d)(p).
4. *Whole fields*: (a) cross field, Delta^(d)(s) = log S1^(d)(s) - log S2^(d)(s); (b) Cd44,
   log lambda^(d)(s) - log(tissue mean of lambda^(d)).

* Inference

- *Primary, joint.* Scalar contrasts (areal averages): 95% equal-tailed posterior interval;
  "credibly different" if it excludes 0. For a scalar, joint and pointwise coincide. Slices and
  whole fields: excursion sets from Monte Carlo draws, =excursions::excursions.mc=, level u = 0,
  alpha = 0.05, positive (type ">") and negative (type "<") separately; slices also get a
  simultaneous 95% band, =excursions::simconf.mc=.
- *Secondary, pointwise.* Pointwise 95% intervals, used only to show where a difference lies.
- *Size, always.* Every result reports the posterior median ratio exp(contrast) and its
  interval, never "different / not different" alone (AOZ decision 6).

* "Looks different" (plug-in reading)

Operational definition: the plug-in ratio r = max(a, b) / min(a, b) >= T, with a, b the regional
averages of the plug-in rate field (published common-minimum field divided by total count). It
concerns LEVEL only (is the field generally higher in one region), not shape or arrangement, and
not whether the two regions follow different generating processes (the model assumes one
stationary field per gene, so the comparison is about the realized field).

Rulings (AOZ, 2026-10-16, judging level only, on =design/threshold-examples.png=):

| panel | pair        | regions                       |    r | AOZ ruling                        |
|-------+-------------+-------------------------------+------+-----------------------------------|
| A     | CNTN2-CNTN1 | box-heart-1 vs box-heart-2    | 1.16 | same                              |
| C     | EGF-CAV1    | anat-heart vs anat-lung       | 1.27 | hard to tell, probably different  |
| E     | NRTN-GFRA1  | box-cord-1 vs box-cord-2      | 1.49 | definitely different              |
| B     | CALM1-FAS   | anat-heart vs anat-thymus     | 1.99 | hard to tell, probably different  |
| D     | NID1-ITGB1  | anat-lung vs anat-thymus      | 2.94 | definitely different              |

Primary threshold: T = 1.25 (between the largest "same", 1.16, and the smallest "probably
different", 1.27). Because the rulings near the boundary were "hard to tell", every tabulation
is also reported at T = 1.5 and T = 2 as sensitivity analyses; these are fixed now, not chosen
after results.

Distribution of r across the 1,477 pairs (plug-in only, =design/plugin-ratios.csv=): median r
ranges from 1.17 (box-cord-1 vs box-cord-2) to 1.71 (anat-lung vs anat-spinal-cord) across the
13 frozen comparisons; the share with r >= 1.25 ranges from 37% to 72%, and with r >= 1.5 from
15% to 59%.

* Naive comparators

0. (Added 2026-10-16, before its results were seen; AOZ approved.) *Decorrelated posterior*: each
   bin's posterior draws are permuted independently across bins, which keeps every bin's marginal
   posterior exactly and removes only the correlation between bins. Regional intervals are then
   recomputed. Reported for every regional instance. Purpose: isolate the effect of ignoring
   spatial correlation from the effect of ignoring uncertainty, which the Wilcoxon comparator
   confounds.

1. *Eyeballing / plug-in*: the threshold above.
2. *Bins treated as independent*: two-sided Wilcoxon rank-sum test, alpha = 0.05, on the plug-in
   rate values of the bins in R1 against R2 (same field); paired Wilcoxon signed-rank on plug-in
   S1 - S2 over the bins of R (cross field).
3. *Pointwise intervals without multiplicity* for slices and whole fields: a position or bin is
   called different if its pointwise 95% interval excludes 0.

* Novelty check (Phase 1) result

=novelty-uncertainty.org= (agent-run search, 2026-10-14; read levels recorded there). No single
published method combining model-based posteriors of expression fields, posteriors of signaling
fields, and joint inference over areal averages, transects and whole fields was found, with the
caveat that the search was limited and many sources were read at abstract level. Partial
precedents: Splotch (Aijo et al., bioRxiv 2019; Bayesian region comparisons of gene expression,
per coefficient, no signaling fields), Mejia et al. (JASA 2020; INLA plus excursion sets, fMRI),
SHADE (PLOS Comput Biol 2026; simultaneous bands along a distance axis, imaging), SpaceBF (2025;
per-spot credible intervals for ligand-receptor co-expression).

* Additions logged 2026-10-21, before any of them was run (AOZ approved)

1. *Coarse-grid excursion sets.* Whole-field contrasts are also computed on 5 x 5-bin blocks
   (200 x 130 bins -> 40 x 26 = 1,040 blocks): within each posterior draw, average the field over
   each block first, then form the contrast, then run =excursions.mc= (u = 0, alpha = 0.05, both
   directions). Block size fixed now: 5 bins is about 1 to 2.5 fitted correlation ranges for the
   pilot genes. Purpose: bin-level joint sets give no credit to structure much larger than the
   correlation range; blocks trade resolution for power while keeping the joint guarantee.
   Regional averages remain the primary test for named structures.
2. *Gene field vs a signaling field containing that gene.* One pair containing CD44 is drawn at
   random (seed 20261021) from the 15 pairs in the pair table that contain CD44, restricted to
   pairs whose plug-in median rate over the whole tissue is at or above the median across all
   1,477 pairs; if none qualify, the draw is from all 15. That signaling field is compared with
   its own tissue average exactly as Cd44 is (same three panels, bin level and block level), so
   the Cd44 figure and its partner differ only in "one gene" vs "minimum of Cd44 and a partner".
3. *"Looks different" at each slice position, bin and block (AOZ's option b).* A position looks
   different if the plug-in map differs from its reference by at least the same factor as for
   regions: |plug-in log ratio| >= log(1.25). References: the slice average (slices), the tissue
   average (single field), the other field (cross field). Cross-tabulated against the joint
   excursion sets; "looks alike but in the excursion set" is reported with its size. Sensitivity
   at 1.5 and 2 as for regions.

* Addition logged 2026-10-21, before it was run (AOZ approved, defaults accepted)

4. *Posterior-draw cluster inference.* For a whole-field contrast X (bins x draws), within each
   draw d: mark the bins with X > u (and separately X < -u), with u = 0; find connected
   components of marked bins (4-neighbor connectivity on the 200 x 130 grid,
   =terra::patches=); set I(s, d) = 1 if bin s lies in a component of at least k = 25 bins (the
   area of one 5 x 5 block). The cluster-level joint set is the largest set E with
   P(I(s) = 1 for all s in E) >= 0.95, from =excursions.mc= on the indicator draws (u = 0.5,
   type ">"). Because clusters are re-formed in every draw, their uncertainty is carried into the
   statement rather than conditioned on (no cluster-then-test double dipping). Run first on the
   three showcase whole-field comparisons (CALM1-KCNQ3 vs CALM1-PDE1A; Cd44 and PKM-CD44 vs their
   tissue averages) and reported beside the bin-level and block-level joint sets.
