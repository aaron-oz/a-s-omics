- "Looks different, naive test agrees, posterior does not support it": 14 of 91. Largest:
  DHH-PTCH1, box-heart-1 vs box-heart-2, plug-in ratio 2.33, posterior median 1.54, 95% interval
  0.40 to 7.13, Wilcoxon p = 5e-19.
- "Looks alike but credibly differs": 1 of 91. WNT4-FZD6, lung vs spinal cord: plug-in ratio 1.20,
  posterior median 1.32, 95% interval 1.05 to 1.66 (size reported per AOZ decision 6).
- The naive Wilcoxon test calls a difference in 79 of 91 instances; the posterior interval
  excludes 1 in 42 of 91.
- Cd44 (gene field, 13 comparisons): every posterior interval is narrow; 10 look different and are
  credible, 3 look alike and are credible. A single abundant gene is far less uncertain than a
  signaling field built from two sparser genes.
- Cross field (CALM1-KCNQ3 vs CALM1-PDE1A, shape-normalized, 10 regions): all 10 look different
  and all 10 have Wilcoxon signed-rank p < 1e-14; the posterior interval includes 1 in 3
  (box-heart-2, box-liver, anat-liver).
- Whole field, same cross-field pair: 2,612 bins pointwise-credible (P >= 0.95, above or below),
  198 bins in the joint 95% excursion sets. Cd44 against its own tissue average: 15,431 pointwise,
  8,825 joint.
- Slices (results-slices-summary.csv): for the four Fig 5 pairs, at most 2 positions per slice
  are in a joint excursion set (WNT4-FZD6 none on any slice), although their plug-in max/min
  ratios along a slice range 2.3 to 38. The three drawn pairs have 0 to 10 positions per slice;
  Cd44 has 17 to 85.

* Limits (state with any use of these numbers)

- Seven signaling fields, three drawn at random and four chosen earlier for the paper's Fig 5; not
  a sample from which rates can be generalized. The systematic run (Phase 4) is for that.
- The 13 region comparisons share regions, so they are not independent questions.
- One tissue section, one platform; INLA's approximation to the joint posterior.
- The plug-in ratio uses the published fields (min of posterior medians), which are biased upward
  against the posterior of the minimum (the 2026-10-08 result); part of the gap between "x" and
  the posterior median in fig-P1 is that bias, not uncertainty.
- D = 1,000 draws not yet checked for Monte Carlo stability of the interval endpoints.
