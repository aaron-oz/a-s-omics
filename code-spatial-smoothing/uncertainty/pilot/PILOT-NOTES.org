* Limits (state with any use of these numbers)

- Seven signaling fields, three drawn at random and four chosen earlier for the paper's Fig 5; not
  a sample from which rates can be generalized. The systematic run (Phase 4) is for that.
- The 13 region comparisons share regions, so they are not independent questions.
- One tissue section, one platform; INLA's approximation to the joint posterior.
- The plug-in ratio uses the published fields (min of posterior medians), which are biased upward
  against the posterior of the minimum (the 2026-10-08 result); part of the gap between "x" and
  the posterior median in fig-P1 is that bias, not uncertainty.
- D = 1,000 draws: Monte Carlo stability checked 2026-10-29 (draws-stability.R, below); adequate for the
  regional intervals. Not yet checked for the whole-field excursion sets.

* Decorrelated-posterior comparator (shuffle-test.R; added to DESIGN.org before running)

Each bin's 1,000 draws permuted independently across bins: every bin keeps its own posterior,
only the between-bin correlation is removed. Results: results-shuffle-regional.csv,
results-shuffle-crossfield.csv; thin black bars in fig-P1.

- Signaling fields, 91 regional instances: credible with the joint posterior 42; with the
  decorrelated posterior 64; Wilcoxon p < 0.05 in 79. All 22 changes go from "not credible" to
  "credible"; none the other way.
- Interval width on the log scale, joint / decorrelated: median 2.69, range 1.93 to 5.97 (boxes
  median 2.53, anatomical regions 2.70). Ignoring the correlation makes the intervals 2 to 6 times
  too narrow, while every bin's own uncertainty is still counted.
- Cd44: 13 credible either way (width ratio median 2.4; its intervals are narrow regardless).
- Cross field, 10 regions: credible 7 joint, 9 decorrelated (width ratio median 2.52).
- Rough reading of the gap between the joint posterior (42) and the Wilcoxon test (79): ignoring
  correlation alone accounts for 22 of the 37 extra calls. The remainder is consistent with also
  discarding each bin's uncertainty, but the Wilcoxon test is a different statistic, so this split
  is indicative, not an exact decomposition.

* Posterior-draw cluster inference, u = 0, k = 25 (cluster-inference.R; logged in DESIGN.org before running)

| comparison                     | cluster-level joint set | bin-level joint set | block-level joint set (in bins) |
|--------------------------------+-------------------------+---------------------+---------------------------------|
| CALM1-KCNQ3 vs CALM1-PDE1A     |                     168 |                 198 |                           1,575 |
| Cd44 vs its tissue average     |                   8,737 |               8,825 |                          17,900 |
| PKM-CD44 vs its tissue average |                   2,019 |               2,084 |                           8,700 |

This variant adds nothing over the bin-level joint sets: its sets are slightly smaller and almost
entirely inside them (154 of 168, 8,734 of 8,737, 2,012 of 2,019). Reason: at u = 0, the bins with
X > 0 in a single draw percolate into large connected regions, so the "component of at least 25
bins" condition almost never binds (median posterior probability that a bin lies in such a
positive component is 0.24 to 0.40 across all bins), and the statement still requires every bin
in the set simultaneously. This is a verdict on this variant (u = 0, k = 25, per-bin indicator),
not on cluster-level inference in general: a version that makes a statement about the cluster
itself, or uses a higher u, could behave differently and has not been run.

* Monte Carlo stability of D = 1,000 (draws-stability.R; written 2026-10-21, run 2026-10-29)

The 91 showcase regional contrasts, 95% interval endpoints recomputed on draws 1-500 and 501-1000
separately. Half-versus-half endpoint difference on the log scale: median 0.025, 95th percentile
0.109, maximum 0.268; relative to the interval width, median 0.045 and maximum 0.226. One of 91
credible calls flips between halves. Each half carries sqrt(2) times the full-D error, so the
full-D Monte Carlo error of an endpoint is roughly half these numbers. Verdict: D = 1,000 is
adequate for the regional intervals.

* Monte Carlo stability of the whole-field excursion sets (wholefield-stability.R, 2026-10-29)

Joint sets of the three Fig 8 comparisons recomputed on draws 1-500 and 501-1000 separately
(results-wholefield-stability.csv; sizes: full / half 1 / half 2 / in both halves):
CALM1-KCNQ3 vs CALM1-PDE1A, bins 198 / 234 / 256 / 133, blocks 63 / 67 / 64 / 53;
Cd44, bins 8,825 / 9,392 / 9,417 / 8,787, blocks 716 / 717 / 720 / 710;
PKM-CD44, bins 2,084 / 2,492 / 2,648 / 2,020, blocks 348 / 359 / 352 / 330.
Half-draw bin-level sets are 15 to 30% larger than the full-draw set for the two sparser
comparisons (a 500-draw joint probability is estimated more coarsely, and the greedy set grows
further) and 6% larger for Cd44; block-level sets differ by under 7%. The full-draw sets are
almost entirely contained in each half-draw set (156 and 165 of 198; 8,792 and 8,802 of 8,825;
2,037 and 2,053 of 2,084). Verdict: bin-level set SIZES for sparse fields carry Monte Carlo error
of order 10 to 20% at D = 1,000 (half the half-vs-full gap); their location is stable. Stated in
the manuscript's Methods.

* Lognormal approximation behind the plug-in-bias section, checked against joint draws (lognormal-check.R, 2026-10-29)

Seven showcase pairs, all 26,000 bins, rate scale (results-lognormal-check.csv). Per-bin
|log(draw median of M) - log(lognormal-approximation median of M)|: median 0.020 to 0.062 by pair,
90th percentile 0.054 to 0.152, maximum 0.19 to 0.41; Pearson correlation over bins 0.997 to 0.999.
For comparison, the Monte Carlo error of a per-bin median from 1,000 draws with log-scale posterior
sd about 1 is roughly 1.25 / sqrt(1000) = 0.04, so most of the disagreement is within draw noise.
The gross overstatement is reproduced (median plug-in over median of M: WNT4-FZD6 59% from draws vs
61% approx; RSPO1-LGR6 66% vs 66%; VEGFA-KDR 24% vs 23%). The share of bins with overstatement above
10% is sensitive where the overstatement is near zero (CALR-ITGA2B 9.3% from draws vs 0.2% approx;
DHH-PTCH1 17% vs 1.8%), part of which is draw noise pushing small values over the cutoff. Verdict:
the approximation is adequate for the plug-in section's purpose; its tail percentages are
approximate to a few points, and the manuscript now says so.

* Prior sensitivity of the regional contrasts (refit-draws-prior.R, prior-sensitivity.R, 2026-10-29; DESIGN.org change log)

The 13 genes of the seven showcase pairs (plus Cd44) refitted with D = 1,000 under, one change at
a time: (a) P(range < 250) = 0.95; (b) P(range < 1000) = 0.95; (c) P(sigma > 1) = 0.05. Draws in
/var/home/aoz/data/a-s-omics-uncertainty/draws-prior-{a,b,c}/. Results
(results-prior-sensitivity.csv), 91 regional contrasts each:
- (a) and (b): 0 of 91 credible calls change; median interval width ratio to baseline 1.002 and
  1.004 (range 0.92 to 1.21); posterior ranges and sigmas unchanged to three figures (the data
  dominate the range prior for these genes).
- (c): 2 of 91 calls change, both from credible to not credible, both with an endpoint already
  near 1 at baseline: CALR-ITGA2B heart box 1 vs liver box (0.46, interval 0.23 to 0.90 -> 0.49,
  0.25 to 1.01) and RSPO1-LGR6 heart vs thymus (1.68, 1.07 to 2.68 -> 1.60, 1.00 to 2.77). Median
  width ratio 0.99 (range 0.67 to 1.15); largest shift of a posterior median ratio 0.21 on the log
  scale; fitted sigma up by a median factor of 1.08 and range down by 0.94 (Fzd6 most: sigma 2.24 ->
  3.07, range 93 -> 62 chip units).
- Decision rule (fixed before running): more than 9 of 91 changes would have required a
  prior-sensitivity statement in the Discussion; 2 changes, of the order of the 1 flip seen between
  halves of the baseline draws, requires one reporting sentence, now in the Discussion.
- Caveat: INLA printed "iterative process seems to diverge, 'vb.correction' is aborted" for several
  of the prior-(a) fits (the fit then proceeds without the variational-Bayes correction). Whether
  the baseline pilot refits printed the same was not recorded; the reproduction check in
  FIDELITY.org passed for them. Not pursued.
