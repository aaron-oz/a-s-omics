* Checks

- Reproduction: 778 of 790 genes match the published fit within Monte Carlo noise on the robust
  check (=check2.csv=); 12 do not (Alox5, Artn, Cmklr1, Cspg4, Fgl1, Il1r2, Ly96, Mchr1, Nxph3,
  Ocln, Pmch, Tlr1; sparse genes). They touch 17 of 1,477 pairs, which are flagged, not dropped;
  every count below is also given without them in =summary.org= (changes are under 1%).
  For those 17 pairs (221 instances), "looks different" was also recomputed from the refit's own
  medians (=flagged-refit-plugin.csv=, AOZ option a, 2026-10-21): 214 of 221 calls agree with the
  published-fit version; "looks different, not credible" is 29 with the refit plug-in vs 31 with
  the published one; median |log| difference between the two plug-in ratios 0.015.
- Pilot vs cluster, the 91 showcase instances (different seeds and machine): 90 of 91 credible
  calls agree; interval endpoints differ by a median of 0.011 on the log scale. The one
  disagreement (NID1-ITGB1, spinal-cord boxes) has a lower endpoint near 1 in both (0.98 vs 1.01).

* Limits

- One tissue section, one platform; INLA's approximation to the joint posterior.
- The 13 region comparisons and the 11,718 cross-field pairs share regions and genes, so counts
  are not independent and are not rates over a population of tissues or pairs.
- "Looks different" is a proxy for eyeballing (the regional-average ratio), set from five
  rulings; hence the three thresholds.
- The plug-in fields are biased upward against the posterior of the minimum, so part of the
  plug-in/posterior gap is that bias.
- Sizes for "looks alike but credible" are mostly near the threshold (median 1.26).
