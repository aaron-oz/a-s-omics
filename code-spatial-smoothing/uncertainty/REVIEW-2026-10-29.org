* 1. What was checked and found sound

- Design frozen before any posterior comparison (=DESIGN.org=, 2026-10-16), additions logged with
  dates before being run (2026-10-21). Regions chosen from the annotation and total counts only
  (=design/design-maps.R= draws no gene field). Showcase pairs drawn with a fixed seed from a
  prespecified rule, or chosen for the original Fig 5 long before. This is the right practice
  and the manuscript says so.
- Fidelity of the refits to the published fits (=pilot/FIDELITY.org=): every row SAME or argued
  harmless; the reproduction check passes for all 17 pilot genes and 778 of 790 systematic genes.
  The first cluster check (=uq-stage1.R=, a z-statistic treating bins as independent) was wrong
  for the reason its replacement states (Monte Carlo error is shared across bins); the replacement
  (=uq-check2.R=) uses median log shift and median interval-width ratio with thresholds set from
  seed-to-seed spread on 7 genes. That is a soft flag, applied as a flag (17 pairs kept and
  reported both ways, changes about 1% or less). Acceptable.
- Interaction-field posterior: =M^(d)(s) = min(lambda_L^(d)(s), lambda_R^(d)(s))= per bin and
  draw (=compare-lib.R::sig_draws=, =uq-stage2.R=). Regional averages and the tissue mean are
  computed within each draw (correct: the average of a minimum is not the minimum of averages).
  Shape normalization divides by the within-draw tissue mean (=uq-stage2.R= stores =tissue_mean=
  per draw; =analyze-systematic.R= divides region means by it draw by draw). Rate scale: the
  published count-scale field divided by =total.count= (checked: =Rpub <- sweep(Mpub, 2,
  tc$total.count, "/")=, bin order asserted equal).
- Decorrelated comparator: =Md <- t(apply(M, 1, sample))= permutes each bin's 1,000 draws
  independently (seeded per pair). Marginals preserved exactly, between-bin correlation removed.
  Correct.
- Joint inference: =excursions::excursions.mc= at u = 0, alpha = 0.05, each direction; pointwise
  calls at the same one-sided level so the joint set is a subset; =simconf.mc= for the band.
  Matches the manuscript's description.
- int16 storage of log draws at resolution 0.001: the cluster check recomputes the reproduction
  statistics from the quantized draws, so the quantization is inside the check. Fine.
- Pilot versus cluster (different seeds, machines, and INLA installations): 90 of 91 credible
  calls agree; the one disagreement sits at an endpoint of 0.98 versus 1.01. Verified in
  =RESULTS.org= (not recomputed today).
- Tabulation (=analyze-systematic.R=): counts in Table 2 of the manuscript match
  =klone/results/summary.org= (11,527; 3,603; 991; 17,320; 14,006; 8,915). Checked by reading.

* 2. New checks run today (2026-10-29)

** 2a. Monte Carlo stability of D = 1,000 draws (=pilot/draws-stability.R=, written 2026-10-21, never run until today)

For the 91 showcase regional contrasts, the 95% interval endpoints were recomputed on draws
1-500 and 501-1000 separately. Half-versus-half endpoint difference on the log scale: median
0.025, 95th percentile 0.109, maximum 0.268; relative to the interval width, median 0.045
(4.5% of the width), maximum 0.226. One of 91 credible calls flips between halves. Since each
half carries sqrt(2) times the full-D error, the full-D Monte Carlo error of an endpoint is
roughly half these numbers. D = 1,000 is adequate for the regional intervals; this is now
recorded in =pilot/PILOT-NOTES.org= and in the manuscript's Methods.

** 2b. A rank test on the observed data rather than on the smoothed map (=klone/raw-wilcoxon.R=, new)

The manuscript's "bins as independent observations" comparator is a Wilcoxon rank-sum test on
the plug-in (smoothed, fitted) field values of the bins in two regions. I asked what the same
test gives on the observed per-bin score, min(y_L, y_R)/N, with no model at all. Result over
the same 19,201 instances: the observed score is zero at a median of 99.8% of bins in a region
(90th percentile of the nonzero share 4.4%; in the 225-bin boxes the median nonzero share is 0).
The test is undefined (both regions all zero) in 3,086 instances, rejects at 0.05 in 7,300
(38%), and rejects in only 483 of the 3,603 "looks different, not credible" instances. It agrees
with the joint posterior on 5,821 of the 8,915 credible instances. So the 90% rejection rate of
the manuscript's Wilcoxon comparator is a property of testing SMOOTHED values as if independent
(which is what is done when a kernel-smoothed or imputed map is tested spot by spot), not of
rank tests on these data in general; on the raw per-bin scores the test is weak, not
anticonservative. The manuscript now says this (Methods, Results, Discussion, Table 2) and the
comparator is named "Wilcoxon test on the plug-in map, bins independent" throughout.

This comparator was added after the other results were known; it is a comparator, not a
selection among results, and the date is logged in =DESIGN.org=.

** 2c. Monte Carlo stability of the whole-field excursion sets (=pilot/wholefield-stability.R=, new)

The joint sets of the three Fig 8 comparisons were recomputed on draws 1-500 and 501-1000.
Bin-level half-draw sets are 15 to 30% larger than the full-draw set for the two sparser
comparisons (CALM1-KCNQ3 vs CALM1-PDE1A: 198 full, 234 and 256 on the halves; PKM-CD44: 2,084,
2,492 and 2,648) and 6% larger for Cd44 (8,825; 9,392 and 9,417); block-level sets differ by
under 7%. The full set is almost entirely inside each half set. So the SIZE of a bin-level set
for a sparse field carries Monte Carlo error of order 10 to 20% at D = 1,000, while its location
is stable; block-level sets are stable in both. The manuscript's Methods now say so, and the
whole-field counts in the Results are described as approximate to that order.

* 3. Issues found, ranked, with what was done

1. (Fixed in text today) The Wilcoxon comparator was described as "treating locations as
   independent" without saying it is applied to the smoothed map. The abstract's sentence "a
   rank test treating locations as independent finds a difference in 90% of comparisons" would
   be read as a statement about naive analysis of the data; section 2b shows it is a statement
   about testing smoothed outputs. The 90% figure stands, with its construct named.
2. (Fixed in text today) "Ignoring the correlation accounts for most of the gap": the gap between
   the Wilcoxon test (90%) and the joint posterior (46%) is 44 points; removing correlation alone
   moves the posterior from 46% to 73%, 27 of the 44 points. "Most" is accurate; the arithmetic
   is now given, with the caveat that the two are different statistics.
3. (Fixed in text today) "With 1,000 draws, that fraction is resolved to 0.001" states the
   granularity of a Monte Carlo estimate, not its precision. The standard error of an estimated
   joint probability near 0.95 from 1,000 draws is about sqrt(0.95 x 0.05 / 1000) = 0.007, and
   the stability check in 2a is the better statement.
4. (Fixed in text today) Levels: the regional "credible" call is two-sided (95% equal-tailed
   interval excludes 1); the pointwise bin and position calls are one-sided (posterior probability
   of exceeding 0 at least 0.95), chosen so that the joint set is a subset; the transect band is
   two-sided 95%. All correct in the code, but the manuscript did not say the pointwise calls are
   one-sided. It does now.
5. (Not fixed; plan item P1) The plug-in bias section (Fig 5) is computed from lognormal
   approximations to the stored marginal posteriors, because it predates the joint draws. The
   joint draws now exist for 17 pilot genes locally and could verify the approximation on the
   seven showcase pairs. Cheap; not done today.
6. (Not fixed; plan item P2) No prior sensitivity analysis. The penalized-complexity priors
   (P(range < 500 chip units) = 0.95, P(sigma > 0.1) = 0.05) are informative, the posterior ranges
   for the example genes are 2 to 9 bins, and the width of a regional contrast interval for a
   sparse gene depends on both. The Discussion names the stationarity assumption but not the
   priors.
7. (Done today, see 2c) Monte Carlo stability of the whole-field excursion sets.
8. (Not fixed; plan item P4) The threshold for "looks different" rests on one author's rulings
   on five maps. The sensitivity rows at 1.5 and 2 cover this, and a second rater (Sam) could rule
   on the same five panels (S11 Fig B) at near-zero cost.
9. (Acceptable as is) Twelve refits differ from the published fits for an unknown reason; flagged,
   not dropped, and the counts move by about 1% or less. The manuscript says so.
10. (Acceptable as is) The 19,201 comparisons share regions and pairs and are not a sample from
    any population; the manuscript says so in Results, Table 2 and Discussion, and the counts are
    presented as descriptions of one section.
11. (Acceptable as is) The model is one stationary Matern field per gene; sharp anatomical
    boundaries are a known misspecification. Discussed with the Velidi et al. citation.
12. (Acceptable as is) "Not credible" means the 95% interval includes 1, not that the regions are
    the same. The manuscript uses "not supported" and "does not establish"; I removed the few
    places where the wording slid toward "no difference".

* 6. Writing: what was changed and why (details in the session report)

The new sections were written in a clipped register (short declarative sentences, numbers
stacked without connective tissue), while the legacy sections are expansive. The revised
text moves the new sections toward AOZ's register as seen in his dissertation: complete
sentences that state the question before the number, the number with its denominator and
period, and the limitation in the same paragraph; roadmap sentences at the start of the
Methods and Results subsections; one term per concept ("interaction field", "plug-in map",
"joint posterior", "credible"). Legacy text was left largely as written except for terminology,
the removal of double-hyphen dashes used as em-dashes, and transitions into the new material.
