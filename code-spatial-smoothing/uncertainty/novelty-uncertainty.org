Acronyms: ST = spatial transcriptomics; LR = ligand-receptor; SPDE = stochastic partial differential equation; INLA = integrated nested Laplace approximation; GP = Gaussian process; CAR = conditional autoregressive; BF = Bayes factor; FDR = false discovery rate; SVG = spatially variable gene; DE = differential expression; CCC = cell-cell communication.

* 1. Bottom line

I found no single published method that does what the revised paper proposes, namely (a) a model-based posterior for each gene's expression field on ST data, (b) posterior fields for LR signaling built from the gene fields, and (c) joint (simultaneous) inference to compare areal averages, 1D transects, and whole fields via excursion sets. Pieces exist separately. The closest gene-field precedent is Splotch (Aijo et al., bioRxiv 2019): a fully Bayesian hierarchical model (zero-inflated Poisson with a CAR spatial term, per-spot UMI size factors, Stan sampling) whose posteriors over anatomical-region coefficients are compared between regions with Savage-Dickey Bayes factors; this covers posterior uncertainty on expression and region comparisons, but the comparisons are element-wise (per coefficient, not joint), there are no transects, no whole-field or excursion-set inference, and no signaling fields. The closest precedent for the inferential machinery is Mejia et al. (JASA 2020), who fit latent fields with INLA and used excursion sets on the joint posterior (via the excursions package) to localize activation in cortical-surface fMRI (abstract only); it is not genomics. The closest precedent for simultaneous bands along a 1D axis is SHADE (Eliason et al., PLOS Comput Biol 2026), which reports simultaneous 95% credible bands on spatial interaction curves between cell types in multiplexed imaging and compares cohorts by whether the bands overlap; it is not expression fields or LR signaling. For LR signaling, the Bayesian ST methods I found (SpaceBF, BATCOM, spacia) give posterior summaries for coefficients, with SpaceBF reporting per-spot credible intervals and masking non-credible spots, but none of those I opened builds a posterior for a signaling field and compares fields jointly. The established LR/CCC tools I opened (CytoSignal preprint, SpatialDM, COMMOT) use permutation or analytical null tests and descriptive comparisons, not posterior fields. Caveat: the search was limited (Section 4), several relevant sources were abstract-only or inaccessible, so absence here is "not found", not "does not exist".

* 3. Per-paper notes

** Splotch (closest for gene fields)
Read: methods/full text via extraction (bioRxiv 2019 preprint). The fetch described a hierarchical zero-inflated Poisson regression with exponential link, a tissue-context term, a CAR local-neighborhood term over a 4-connected spot graph, and a spot-level term; size factors from per-spot UMI totals; full Bayesian inference by adaptive HMC in Stan. Region differences were quantified with Savage-Dickey Bayes factors, "element-wise" on coefficients; the extraction said no BF threshold was given and credible intervals for DE calls were not reported in the main text. Differences from the revised paper: CAR on the spot lattice rather than a Matern SPDE field on a mesh; per-coefficient comparisons rather than joint inference; no signaling fields; no transects. A peer-reviewed version was not found in Europe PMC (only the preprint), so cite as a preprint unless the authors know otherwise.

** Mejia et al. 2020 and Bolin and Lindgren (inferential machinery)
Mejia et al. (abstract only) fit latent fields with INLA and identify activation regions with an excursions set "based on the joint posterior distribution of the latent fields, rather than the marginal distribution at each location". This is the same inferential recipe the revised paper uses, applied to fMRI. Bolin and Lindgren 2015 (JRSS-B) describe excursion sets and contour credible regions for latent Gaussian models and relate the problem to "multiple significance testing" (abstract). The 2018 JSS paper documents the excursions package, which per the arXiv abstract also computes simultaneous confidence bands.

** SHADE (simultaneous bands on 1D curves)
Read via extraction. Simultaneous 95% credible bands are used for smooth spatial interaction curves along a distance axis, and groups are compared by whether the bands exclude zero or overlap. Closest analog to the transect analysis; differences: multiplexed cell-type imaging, Stan not INLA, interaction curves not expression or signaling fields.

** SpaceBF (closest for LR with posterior intervals)
Read preprint methods via extraction (first 100,000 of 218,014 chars). Local and global slopes between two molecules with credible intervals; probability of direction as a p-value analogue; non-credible spots greyed out. Negative binomial likelihood with Polya-Gamma augmentation, not Poisson with an offset, and the two-molecule slope is not a pointwise-minimum signaling field. The extraction said there is no formal region test and no joint test across locations.

** BayesTME
Read preprint methods via extraction. "Full quantification of uncertainty" over latent variables; posterior uncertainty used for Bayesian-FDR feature selection. No region-vs-region test described in the text read.

** Velidi, Wei, Nathoo 2026
Read via extraction. Uses R-INLA with SPDE Matern models on 13 Visium datasets (the abstract text I saw said 12; the extraction said 13, so the count is unverified) to compare stationary and non-stationary covariance per gene via approximate Bayes factors. Methodological neighbor (INLA/SPDE on ST) but no field comparison.

** CytoSignal, SpatialDM, COMMOT (LR tools)
All three provide null-hypothesis-based significance (permutation or analytical), not posterior fields. SpatialDM tests differential communication across samples with a likelihood ratio test on global scores and, in the extraction, notes few replicates make this hard and suggests a Bayesian approach as a possible mitigation. COMMOT's p-values are at the cluster level; spot-level scores had no stated uncertainty in the text read. CytoSignal (preprint) uses permutation of locations plus spatial FDR and Wilcoxon tests between significant and non-significant cells within clusters; the Wilcoxon tests are the kind of independence-assuming comparison the revised paper contrasts against. CytoSignal uses the same Stereo-seq mouse embryo atlas (per the preprint text; the final Nature Genetics version was not opened and may differ).

** C-SIDE
Journal abstract says it gives "valid uncertainty quantification" for DE; the preprint version read used z-tests from Fisher-information standard errors and a pixel-wise likelihood product, and the extraction found no discussion of spatial autocorrelation. Whether the journal version differs was not checked.

** BATCOM, spacia
BATCOM provides an HMC posterior but converts it to a Wald-type test with FDR; it works at the cell-type-pair level, not on fields. spacia was abstract only.

- INLAomics (bioRxiv 10.1101/2025.05.02.651831): fetch failed (file too large); only a search-result snippet seen, which described a CAR-based INLA multi-omic model. Not characterized here.
- BOOST-GP and spacia full texts: blocked by a PMC human-verification page; abstracts only.
- Nature Genetics CytoSignal and Nature Methods COMMOT publisher pages: redirected to login; not opened (preprints read instead).
- SpNeigh (bioRxiv 2025): appeared only in a search-result snippet; not opened.

* 4. What appears not to have been done (candidate novelty, stated cautiously)

Based only on what I found and read, the following combination was not found in any source I opened:
1. A single-gene expression field model for ST with a Matern SPDE latent field, Poisson likelihood and total-UMI offset, fit with INLA, used as the basis for reporting a full posterior of the rate field. Closest: Splotch (CAR, Stan, ZIP) and Velidi et al. (R-INLA SPDE on Visium, for covariance model comparison, not rate-field inference).
2. A posterior for an LR signaling field constructed from the ligand and receptor posterior fields (pointwise minimum). The LR tools opened give null-test p-values (CytoSignal, SpatialDM, COMMOT) or posterior summaries of regression coefficients (SpaceBF, BATCOM), not a signaling-field posterior.
3. Joint posterior inference (excursion sets and simultaneous bands) applied to ST expression or signaling fields. The recipe exists in neuroimaging (Mejia et al. 2020, abstract only) and simultaneous bands exist in imaging-based spatial interaction curves (SHADE); I found neither applied to ST expression or LR signaling.
4. A side-by-side comparison of areal averages, line transects with simultaneous bands, and whole-field excursion sets against naive readings (map eyeballing, independence-assuming tests such as the Wilcoxon tests used in CytoSignal's pipeline or the z-tests in C-SIDE as described in the preprint).

Caveats on this conclusion:
- Many sources were abstract-only; a method that does these things inside its Methods section could be invisible to me.
- The fetch tool covers only the first 100,000 characters of long pages, so later methods and supplementary text were not read for several papers (CytoSignal, SpaceBF, C-SIDE, COMMOT, BayesTME).
- Europe PMC search indexes titles, abstracts and some full text; recent preprints and non-biomedical statistics venues (spatial statistics, Annals of Applied Statistics, Biometrics) may be under-covered.
- Preprint-only and 2026 entries may have changed since they were indexed.
