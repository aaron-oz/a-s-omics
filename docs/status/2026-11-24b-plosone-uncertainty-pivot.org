* Decisions made, and why
- Fig 1 glyphs on mRNA density and binding density, none on protein density (AOZ): the model estimates the mRNA rate fields, and protein is an assumed proportional proxy.
- "plug-in map" is the single name (AOZ, on the agent's recommendation): the posterior median map is also a point estimate, so "point-estimate map" would blur the contrast the Results section draws.
- An AI-use disclosure is included, naming Claude Code with Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.2 (AOZ). AOZ wrote the sentence on how output was evaluated.
- The threshold of 1.25 for "looks different", set from AOZ's rulings on five example maps, stays as is and Sam was not asked to rule on the maps (AOZ: five rulings is weak, but it should not change now).
- Discussion order and cuts (AOZ): the two paragraphs on tissue biology, the "All models are wrong" quotation, the closing sentence about working with the community, and the lung-adaptation sentence were removed. The method-by-method survey stays in the Discussion, tightened.
- Region figure uses AOZ's layout over the agent's one-row alternative (AOZ).
- The abstract may stay a little over 300 words for now, because the clustering sentence may be cut (AOZ). The 300-word limit is from the agent's memory of the PLOS ONE guidelines and was not checked.
