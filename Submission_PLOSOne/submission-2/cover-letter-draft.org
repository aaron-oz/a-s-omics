Dear Dr. D'Acquisto,

Please find attached our revised manuscript, "Spatial Modeling of Tissues for Morphogenic Field
Analysis" (PONE-D-26-04349), together with a point-by-point response to the reviewers and a
version of the manuscript with changes marked. We thank you and the reviewers for a careful
review, and you for the extensions that made a thorough revision possible.

The response letter addresses every comment from the journal, the academic editor and both
reviewers. In summary:

- At Reviewer 2's request we added controls. Refitting the whole pipeline on spatially permuted
  data collapses the tissue domains; the domains agree well above chance with the anatomical
  annotation published independently for the same section; and, in the third control the
  reviewer suggested, shuffling which receptor is paired with which ligand does not make the
  domains less coherent. We report that last result in full, together with the limit it places
  on our conclusions.
- We rewrote the model specification, added a supplementary table mapping every mathematical
  symbol to the code that implements it, documented the analysis repository for reproduction,
  and rebuilt the figures from scripts at the journal's page size.
- In re-deriving our results from the archived outputs we found and corrected several errors of
  our own, including two feature counts, the direction of one figure caption, and two claims
  in the Results that our saved data did not support. These are listed under "Additional
  corrections" in the response letter.
- We have narrowed the paper's claims to match the evidence: the method is demonstrated on one
  field of view, and the inferred fields estimate the potential for signaling rather than
  measuring it.
